## Short description
A personal Android TV home with wallpaper colors and optional recommendations.

## Full description
Make your Android TV home feel like yours.

Choose the apps you want on Home and browse them in a simple horizontal row. Find everything else in All Apps, with search and a separate section for TV utilities.

Personalize your background with a photo, gradient or solid color. Match menus and focus accents to your wallpaper, or choose a warm or cool theme. Adjust color intensity, icon size, clock size and app labels.

Optional recommendations stay under your control. Enable them per app, choose a featured source, and browse an immersive carousel when compatible apps share content. Artwork, descriptions, title links and video previews depend on the source app and device; subscriptions do not guarantee integration.

Navigate with your TV remote. Keep input shortcuts and settings within reach, and turn optional recent-app shortcuts on or off.

Luma Home has no account sign-in, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. Preferences and imported images are stored on your TV. Optional artwork and video use the URLs supplied by enabled content sources.

Designed for Android TV and Google TV. Features such as default Home selection, shared recommendations and input shortcuts vary by device. Luma Home does not provide movies or subscriptions and is not affiliated with streaming services or Google.
