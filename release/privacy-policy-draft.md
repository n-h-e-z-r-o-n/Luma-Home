## Information on your TV
Luma Home reads launchable installed apps so it can display and open shortcuts. It saves your selected Home apps, layout preferences, names, artwork settings, wallpaper colors and input visibility in private app storage. If you enable recent apps, it records only apps opened through Luma Home; you can clear this history in Settings. It does not read a system-wide usage history.

Photos you select through an available system/file picker are resized and copied into the app's private storage. Wallpaper colors are derived locally. Luma Home does not upload these imported photos to a developer-operated service. Replacing a photo does not necessarily delete all previously imported image files; clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes its private copies.

## Optional shared content
When enabled, Luma Home reads recommendations made available by compatible installed apps through the TV content provider. It uses supplied artwork, descriptions, title links and supported preview-video URLs. Availability and access depend on the source app and device. Luma Home does not ask for your streaming passwords or manage subscriptions.

Retrieving remote artwork or video connects your TV to the provider of that URL. The provider receives network information such as the request and IP address, and its own privacy policy applies. Opening a title sends the supplied link to the corresponding app. Other apps handle their own accounts, playback and data practices. Content URLs may contain identifiers supplied by those providers; these integrations must be reviewed for the final Google Play Data safety declaration.

## Developer services and permissions
The current build has no developer-operated backend, account system, analytics SDK or advertising SDK. Internet access supports optional remote content. TV-listings access supports shared recommendations where the platform permits it. File access is supplied through the selected picker; the app does not request broad storage permission. Input shortcuts use the TV's exposed input information.

## Your choices and deletion
You can disable recommendations globally or per app, disable the featured area, clear recent app history and change themes. To remove all locally stored settings and imported files, use Android's app-data clearing option or uninstall Luma Home. This does not remove data held independently by streaming apps or content providers.
