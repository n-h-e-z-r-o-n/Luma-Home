# Branding assets

The user-supplied 136x136 PNG is retained exactly as icon-source.png and packaged as the legacy icon and adaptive foreground artwork. No redesign of that APK icon was made.

The matching TV banner was produced with the built-in image-generation tool, then copied into this project. Prompt: "Create a clean Android TV launcher banner, exact aspect ratio 16:9, for the existing app Luma Home. Use the attached icon as the identity reference: faithfully preserve its frosted gray house outline in front of a glowing warm amber sun, on an almost-black charcoal background. Place that exact recognizable house-and-sun mark on the left, with generous margins, and the text 'Luma Home' in large crisp white medium-weight sans serif to the right. No slogan, no other text, no framing, no device mockup, no rounded outside corners; edge-to-edge dark background. The graphic should be simple and highly legible downsampled to 320 by 180 pixels. This is an adaptation of the provided icon into a horizontal app banner, not a redesign of the logo."

Master: tv-banner-master.png. Resource export: ../res/drawable-xhdpi/tv_banner.png. Play artwork drafts use the sizes in their filenames. Mechanical resizing/letterboxing preserves proportions. The 512px store icon is upscaled from the original; it does not contain additional source detail.
